This choose-your-own-adventure story drops you into the shoes of Diane, a new grad confronting toxic leadership in a high-pressure product launch. Through dozens of branching passages you can consult HR, document abuse, back up colleagues, or even escalate to legal action—each decision unlocks distinct endings and explores the nuance of accountability.

Built entirely in Twine and exported as a single-page HTML experience, the piece combines forum threads, recorded conversations, and internal politics to bring the ACM ethics case study to life. It serves as an interactive teaching tool for CMPT 376W, letting students rehearse ethical standpoints and see how workplace culture can shift based on the actions they take.
