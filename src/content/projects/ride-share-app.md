Built for the SFU Ride Share hackathon, this Expo-managed app captures the core onboarding and home experience for matching drivers and riders. It greets users with profile-aware messaging, preserves role preferences in Zustand stores, and wires quick actions to planned ride and chat flows.

The repo doubles as a testing playground: lint, type-check, Jest, and Playwright scripts keep UI and navigation paths reliable, while service stubs document how Firebase and maps integrations will land in subsequent iterations.
