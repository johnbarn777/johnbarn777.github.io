House is a production-ready roommate assistant that fuses onboarding, authentication, and real-time collaboration into a single React Native experience. New members can create or join a house in seconds, manage secure email and Google logins, and land in a home hub that surfaces the tasks, expenses, and updates that matter most.

Under the hood the app leans on Firebase Auth, Firestore, and Cloud Messaging to keep every device synchronized. Custom Firestore schemas power chore assignment, role-based permissions, swipe-to-edit task flows, and push notification deep links. The project also lays out the roadmap for analytics, premium tiers, and offline support, showcasing a full-stack vision for running a modern shared-living platform on iOS and Android.
